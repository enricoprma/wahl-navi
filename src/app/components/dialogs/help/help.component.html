How does Wahl-Navi work?

Answer each statement with Agree, Neutral, or Disagree. You can skip statements or give them double weight.

At the end, your answers are compared with the parties' positions.

What to do

  1. Read the statement carefully.
  2. Select Agree, Neutral, or Disagree.
  3. Use Skip, Back, or Double weight when needed.
  4. Review your party matches on the results page.

Notes

  • You can return to earlier statements and change your answer.
  • Skipped statements do not count toward the result.
  • Double-weighted statements count twice in the calculation.
